About

I'm Vaiva Kalnikaitė — a design leader, researcher, and founder with over 15 years of experience shaping human experiences across AI, enterprise, and emerging technology.

I'm currently Head of UX at Amazon Web Services, leading design for agentic AI and human-in-the-loop systems. I'm also the founder of Dovetailed, an award-winning UX and innovation studio based in the UK and US.

I hold a PhD in Human-Computer Interaction from the University of Sheffield and am a Fellow of the Royal Society of Arts. My research has been published at ACM CHI and CSCW, earning three Best Paper Awards.

I'm based in Seattle, USA, with roots in Cambridge, UK and Vilnius, Lithuania.
